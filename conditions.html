What we treat

The things people actually come in with.

Every condition on this page is one an ACTIONPhysio patient has written to the clinic about. We’d rather list six things honestly than twenty we can’t point to. If what you’re dealing with isn’t here, ring and ask — the answer is often yes.

Lower back pain

The most common reason people find us. It might be a back that seized up years ago and never properly settled, a desk-bound back that aches by mid-afternoon, or a back that quietly stopped someone doing the thing they love.

  • A full assessment before anyone touches you — what hurts, when it started, what it stops you doing
  • Hands-on treatment to settle the pain and free up the movement
  • A short home programme so the gain holds between sessions
  • Review as you go, and honest talk about how long it’s likely to take

Ask about this

Knee pain

Stairs, squatting, kneeling, running. Knees rarely fail on their own — more often they are paying for something the hip or the ankle isn’t doing. We look at the whole chain, then load the knee back up in a way it can take.

  • Working out what the knee is compensating for
  • Restoring range before adding strength
  • Graded loading, so you build back rather than flare it up
  • Clear markers of progress you can feel for yourself

Ask about this

Neck, shoulders & posture

Tight upper traps, weak rhomboids, shoulders that sit forward, a neck that aches by the end of the working day. Very treatable, and very prone to returning if only the sore bit gets treated.

  • Manual therapy to release what’s tight
  • Strengthening what’s gone weak — usually the mid-back
  • Practical changes to how you sit and work
  • Simple corrective measures you can do at home

Ask about this

Ankle & foot

Rolled ankles that never quite recovered, stiff ankles, feet that hurt on the first step out of bed. Ankles are forgiving if they get proper rehab and stubborn if they don’t.

  • Getting the range back first
  • Then strength, then balance — in that order
  • Return-to-activity work, whether that’s hiking or just stairs
  • What to do if it goes again

Ask about this

Wrist, hand & elbow

The joints that make ordinary life hard when they stop working — washing, carrying, gripping, opening a jar. Often overlooked, and often the quickest to improve.

  • Hands-on treatment for the joint and the soft tissue around it
  • Graded loading to rebuild usable grip strength
  • Adjustments to the daily tasks that keep aggravating it

Ask about this

Staying mobile with age

A large part of our work is with clients in their fifties, sixties and beyond who have no intention of slowing down. Nobody here will promise miracles — but guided, consistent movement does slow the drift, and our clients feel it.

  • Movement assessment without the gym-floor nonsense
  • A programme built for what you actually want to keep doing
  • Stretching and strength work you can sustain
  • Regular review as things change
