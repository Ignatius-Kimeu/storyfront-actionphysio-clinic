What we treat

Most people arrive with one of these.

Every one of the conditions below is something an ACTIONPhysio patient has written to the clinic about. If yours isn’t listed, it doesn’t mean we can’t help — ring and ask.

Lower back pain

The one we see most. Desk-bound backs, backs that seized up two years ago, backs that stopped someone hiking. Assessment first, then hands-on work and a plan you can hold onto.

Knee pain

Stairs, squats, kneeling, running. We work out what the knee is compensating for — often it’s the hip or the ankle — and load it back up properly.

Neck, shoulders & posture

Tight traps, weak rhomboids, a neck that aches by 3pm. Manual therapy to settle it, then the corrective work that stops it coming straight back.

Ankle & foot

Rolled ankles, stiff ankles, feet that hurt on the first step out of bed. Restoring the range first, then the strength and balance that protect it.

Wrist, hand & elbow

Joints that make ordinary jobs hard — washing, carrying, gripping. Hands-on treatment plus graded loading to get usable strength back.

Staying mobile with age

For clients in their fifties, sixties and beyond who want to keep hiking, keep gardening, keep sleeping through the night. Guided movement, reviewed as you go.
