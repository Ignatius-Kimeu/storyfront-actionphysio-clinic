About the clinic

Two physiotherapists, one small clinic, 53 five-star reviews.

ACTIONPhysio Clinic works out of Ngong Lane Plaza in Woodley. The aim, in the clinic’s own words, is to “enhance the overall physical health, fitness and quality of life of all clients with no limit to age” — by treating them and by teaching them.

Pauline Mutwoki

Physiotherapist

The name that appears in most of the clinic’s testimonials. Pauline takes the WhatsApp line herself, and her clients write about the same two things over and over: careful assessment, and the encouragement to keep showing up.

She has spoken on Spice FM’s After Hours on “How to build yourself again after pain.”

Call 0723 205509

Hellen

Physiotherapist

The clinic’s second physiotherapist, and the other half of the pair clients name when they write in. “Hellen and Pauline, you are doing amazing work,” as one client put it on Instagram after her ankle came good.

Between them they run every appointment at the clinic and every home visit.
