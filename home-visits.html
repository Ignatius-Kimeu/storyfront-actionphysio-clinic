Home visits

We come to you.

Some of the people who need physiotherapy most are exactly the people who can least face the journey to get it. So we treat at home too — the same assessment, the same hands, your own front room.

Where we visit

Woodley and the neighbourhoods around it.

The clinic sits at Ngong Lane Plaza in Woodley, and home visits run out from there across the neighbourhoods nearby.

  • Woodley & along Ngong Road
  • Kileleshwa
  • Lavington
  • Karen

Somewhere else in Nairobi? Ask anyway. If it’s workable, Pauline will say so; if it isn’t, she’ll tell you that too rather than waste your time.

Practicalities

What a home visit needs from you.

Very little, in truth. A bit of clear floor and somewhere to sit.

Space to work

A couple of square metres of clear floor and a firm chair or bed is plenty. We bring what else is needed.

Clothes you can move in

Whatever lets us see and reach the area we’re treating comfortably.

Anyone who helps you

If a family member or carer supports you day to day, it’s useful to have them there for the home programme.

Your reports

Scans, referral letters, discharge notes — anything you already have.

Clinic or home?

Honestly, either works.

The clinic has the equipment and the treatment couch; home has everything familiar and no traffic. Plenty of our clients do both — starting at home and coming in once moving is easier. Say which you’d prefer on the form and we’ll talk it through.

Ngong Lane Plaza, Ground Floor, Woodley — off Ngong Road. Treatment couch, therapy equipment, weights, bands and mat space. Mon–Fri 8:00–17:00, Sat 8:00–14:00.

At home

Woodley, Kileleshwa, Lavington, Karen. Same physiotherapist, same assessment, and the home programme taught in the room where you’ll actually be doing it.
